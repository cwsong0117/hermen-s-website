Project 03

NGEAR

A sports gear e-commerce site built with plain layered PHP and MySQL, running locally on XAMPP. Members can shop, check out with Stripe (test mode), use vouchers and QR codes, keep a wishlist, leave reviews and chat live with admins. Admins get a separate back-office with two-factor login for products, stock, orders, refunds and analytics, and the code was recently hardened with security fixes.

Project 04

TREK

A Flutter app for planning and managing trips in Malaysia. Enter a destination, dates, budget and preferences and Google Gemini builds a day-by-day itinerary, with photos from Wikipedia and Wikimedia and help finding flights, trains and buses between places. During the trip it tracks your spending: receipts are read with OCR, overspending is detected and the budget is reallocated across the remaining activities. Gemini is called through a Supabase Edge Function, so no API key ships in the app.

  • Flutter
  • Dart
  • Supabase
  • Gemini
  • OCR
